Our Story

A Family Story

It all began in a small shop in Damascus. In 1971, my grandfather, Mohammad Khaled Tayara, started his journey crafting leather bags in the Salhieh district. From that moment, craftsmanship became part of our family's identity. As children, my brother and I spent hours in his workshop — watching, learning, and falling in love with the smallest details. That passion never left us. We grew up and turned to technology, spending more than 15 years in software and artificial intelligence. But the love of the craft never left us. Today, we return to our roots in our own way: blending natural leather with wood and mother-of-pearl to create contemporary pieces, each unique and full of detail. Muhanna Craft is not merely a set of products — it is a family story, a heritage, and a passion passed down through generations.

A message from the founder: Omar Muhanna

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the pieces fully handmade?

Yes. Every Muhanna Craft piece is fully handcrafted from walnut wood, natural leather and lustrous mother-of-pearl, with the mother-of-pearl precisely inlaid within the wood.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping is within 7 working days for most pieces. Handbags take up to 40 working days because they are entirely hand-stitched.

Are pieces made to order?

Yes. Each product is handcrafted for you upon order, which is why every piece differs slightly from the next.

Can pieces be personalised with a name or phrase?

Yes — keychains and car ornaments can be customised on request with names or phrases in Kufic calligraphy. Message us on WhatsApp for customisation details.

Do you offer corporate gifting in bulk?

Yes. Our business and hospitality pieces are available as corporate gifts in volume, with customisation available. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss quantities and details.

How do I care for the wood and mother-of-pearl?

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid excess water, chemical cleaners and prolonged direct sunlight to preserve the lustre of the mother-of-pearl and the colour of the walnut.

Do you ship outside the UAE?

We ship within the UAE and across the GCC. For international orders, message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm details and cost.

Where is Muhanna Craft based?

Muhanna Craft is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Orders and enquiries are handled directly over WhatsApp.
